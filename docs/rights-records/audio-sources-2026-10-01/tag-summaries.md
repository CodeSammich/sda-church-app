# Tag summaries of WordProject audio files

What every file's tags say about who made and owns each recording, the evidence behind
[WordProject audio in other languages](../../LEGAL.md#wordproject-audio-in-other-languages-backups-only)
and [WordProject Spanish audio](../../LEGAL.md#wordproject-spanish-audio-reina-valera-1909).
Generated from the files downloaded from WordProject on September 30 and October 1, 2026.
Each row counts the files with that combination of album and artist tags and any
copyright (`TCOP`), owner (`TOWN`), publisher (`TPUB`), comment (`COMM`), or web
address (`WXXX`) tags. An empty cell means the tag is absent.
