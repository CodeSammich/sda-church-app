App Support

New York Chinese SDA Church is the official app of the New York Chinese Seventh-day Adventist Church, for iPhone and Android.

Contact us

For help with the app, or to report a problem, email technology@nyccsda.org or call (718) 205-8618. Tell us your device and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

联系我们 / 聯絡我們：technology@nyccsda.org

Common questions

Change the language, text size, or theme. Open the You tab. The app is available in English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and Spanish.

Read two translations together, or show pinyin. In the Bible, tap the translation button at the top, then turn on Dual-language reading or Show pinyin above Chinese.

The Bible or bulletin won't load. Bible text, audio, and the bulletin need an internet connection. Check your connection and try again.

Bible audio isn't available. Audio is available for the Chinese Union Version (Traditional and Simplified) and the Berean Standard Bible.

Accounts and giving. The app needs no account. The giving page's online option opens the church's AdventistGiving page in your browser; the app itself takes no payments.

Privacy

The app collects no personal data and shows no ads. Read the privacy policy.
