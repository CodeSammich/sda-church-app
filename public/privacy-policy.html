Privacy Policy

New York Chinese SDA Church (the “app”)
Last updated: September 2026

1. What the app does

NY Chinese SDA is a church community app with Bible reading and audio, worship information, hymn and library navigation, Sabbath School resources, church information, and an external giving link. The app does not require a user account for ordinary use. It does not include advertising, analytics, public user profiles, chat, or user-generated posting. Authorized church contributors may submit bulletin information through linked Google Forms outside the app.

2. Church bulletin information

Authorized church schedule managers enter worship assignments and participant names into restricted, church-managed Google Sheets. Authorized form submitters provide weekly worship-program details through Google Forms; the restricted response Sheet may record a submitter’s email address.

A Google Apps Script service returns only an allowlisted bulletin response. Before publication, Latin-script names are shortened to a first name and last initial. Unsupported non-Latin names are replaced with a privacy placeholder. Full names, form submitter email addresses, and other non-allowlisted fields are not returned by the public bulletin service. Shortened names may still identify people within the church community and are treated as personal information.

3. Device-local storage

The app may store settings, saved verse references, cached Bible selections, and privacy-filtered bulletin data in local app or browser storage. This data is not synced to a church account. Web users can remove it by clearing the site’s browser data. iOS and Android users can remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage using the operating system’s app settings.

4. Network services and connection data

The app requests Bible text, Bible-audio metadata or files, sunset times, cover images, and privacy-filtered bulletin data from external services over HTTPS. GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Google Workspace/Apps Script, HelloAO, fetch(bible), Audio Power, Adventist Connect, Chinese Union Mission services, and the sunrise-sunset service may process ordinary connection metadata such as an IP address, user agent, request path, and request time for delivery, security, or service operations. The app does not receive or store those providers’ server logs. Each provider handles information under its own applicable terms and privacy policy.

5. External links and giving

The app links to AdventistGiving, YouTube, Spotify, Zoom, HymnsForWorship.org, zgaxr.com, EGW Writings, Sabbath School services, and Google Forms. The giving button opens AdventistGiving outside the app. Payment details and any donation receipts are handled by that service and the receiving organization, not by this app. External providers may receive ordinary connection information when their pages or media are opened or loaded; their own privacy policies apply. When you choose to share a Bible verse, the selected text is passed to the operating system share sheet and the app you choose; this app does not receive the recipient’s information.

6. Device permissions and requests

The native app uses audio playback, including background playback, and may read the device light sensor to adjust its theme locally. It does not request device location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or notifications.

The app does not create user accounts or maintain personal server profiles, so there is no account to delete. To request correction or removal of church-managed bulletin information, email pastor@nyccsda.org. The church handles requests according to applicable law and its administrative retention practices.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact pastor@nyccsda.org.
