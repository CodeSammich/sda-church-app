Privacy Policy

New York Chinese SDA Church (the “app”)
Last updated: October 2026

1. What the app does

NY Chinese SDA is a church community app with Bible reading and audio, worship information, hymn and library navigation, Sabbath School resources, church information, and an external giving link. The app does not require a user account for ordinary use. It does not include advertising, analytics, public user profiles, chat, or user-generated posting. Authorized church schedule managers maintain bulletin information in a restricted staff-managed Google Sheet outside the app.

2. Church bulletin information

Authorized church schedule managers enter worship assignments and participant names into restricted, church-managed Google Sheets. Final owners maintain weekly worship-program details in the Sabbath Sermon Data tab; the source Sheet may record account activity permitted by the church’s Workspace settings.

A Google Apps Script service returns only an allowlisted bulletin response. Before publication, Latin-script full names are shortened to a first name and last initial. A single-word Latin-script name may appear as entered, and unsupported non-Latin names are replaced with a privacy placeholder. Full names, account metadata, and other non-allowlisted fields are not returned by the public bulletin service. Shortened names may still identify people within the church community and are treated as personal information.

This information is used to communicate worship assignments and weekly program details. The church controls access to the source Sheets through Google Workspace and keeps source data according to its administrative practices.

3. Caching and device-local storage

The bulletin service briefly caches its privacy-filtered responses to reduce Sheet reads. The app may store settings, saved verse references, cached Bible selections, library cover links, and privacy-filtered bulletin data in local app or browser storage. This data is not synced to a church account. Web users can remove it by clearing the site’s browser data. iOS and Android users can remove it by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage using the operating system’s app settings.

4. Network services and connection data

The app requests Bible text, Bible audio, Sabbath School lesson information, cover images, and privacy-filtered bulletin data from external services over HTTPS. These include GitHub Pages and Cloudflare (for the web version), Google Workspace and Apps Script (the bulletin), HelloAO and fetch(bible) (Bible text), Adventist Connect, the Internet Archive, Audio Power, and openbible.com (Bible audio), Adventech (Sabbath School lessons), and EGW Writings and the Chinese Union Mission (library cover thumbnails). These services may process ordinary connection metadata such as an IP address, user agent, request path, and request time for delivery, security, or service operations. The app does not receive or store those providers’ server logs. Each provider handles information under its own applicable terms and privacy policy.

5. External links and giving

The app links to external sites, such as AdventistGiving, YouTube, Spotify, Zoom, HymnsForWorship.org, zgaxr.com, EGW Writings, Sabbath School lesson sites and PDFs such as Alive in Jesus, WordProject, and church and denominational websites. Library books open at their sources, such as EGW Writings, Project Gutenberg, the Internet Archive, HathiTrust, and Chapel Library. The giving button opens AdventistGiving outside the app. Payment details and any donation receipts are handled by that service and the receiving organization, not by this app. External providers may receive ordinary connection information when their pages or media are opened or loaded; their own privacy policies apply, and the church does not receive or store information they collect. When you choose to share a Bible verse, the selected text is passed to the operating system share sheet and the app you choose; this app does not receive the recipient’s information.

6. Device permissions and requests

The native app uses audio playback, including background playback. Its Sunrise/Sunset theme uses sunrise and sunset times for New York, calculated on the device, not the device's location or sensors. It does not request device location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or notifications.

The app does not create user accounts or maintain personal server profiles, so there is no account to delete. To request correction or removal of church-managed bulletin information, email technology@nyccsda.org. The church handles requests according to applicable law and its administrative retention practices.

7. Privacy laws

The app’s data minimization is informed by privacy principles found in laws such as the CCPA and GDPR, but it does not by itself guarantee legal compliance. Which laws apply depends on the deploying organization, its users, and its data practices. This policy is updated when the app’s data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact technology@nyccsda.org.
